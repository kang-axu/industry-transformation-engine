## Comparative Company Study

Key companies under study:
- Stone & Webster
- Bechtel
- Fluor
- Jacobs

Questions:
- What created their first advantage?
- When did that advantage become standardized?
- How did they migrate?
- How did they manage fixed-price and project risk?
- How did they turn project experience into organizational knowledge?
- What separated survivors from failures?
- What can AI-era transformation companies learn from those forks?

Research findings should not become ITE doctrine until they survive comparison with real modern projects.
